03 / INSIDE THE LEARNING PROCESS

Make something.
Understand it.
Make it better.

You learn by doing the work, reviewing your choices and trying again. Our flagship programme brings this approach into an offline studio setting.

See the learning journey ↗
  1. 01

    Start with the shot

    Practise camera handling, lighting, posing and wedding shoot simulations. Build an understanding of the footage before you edit it.

  2. 02

    Shape the story

    Work with photos, album sequences and video timelines. Explore pacing, sound, colour and the details that hold a story together.

  3. 03

    Finish with intention

    Bring your learning into a final wedding film, take part in a critique session and put together your portfolio showreel.
