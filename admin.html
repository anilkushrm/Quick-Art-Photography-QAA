🎥 OBS Studio 1080p 60fps Setup Cheat-Sheet

Premiere Pro aur DaVinci Resolve timeline ko 100% Original Crystal Clear HD me dikhane ke liye OBS me ye settings rakhein:

1. Video Settings (Settings ➔ Video):
• Base (Canvas) Resolution: 1920x1080
• Output (Scaled) Resolution: 1920x1080
• Downscale Filter: Lanczos (Sharpened scaling, 36 samples)
• Common FPS Values: 60 FPS (Editing timeline smooth chalegi)
2. Output Streaming (Settings ➔ Output ➔ Streaming):
• Output Mode: Advanced
• Encoder: NVIDIA NVENC H.264 (ya Apple Silicon Hardware Encoder)
• Rate Control: CBR
• Bitrate: 6000 Kbps (High Definition for Color Grading)
• Keyframe Interval: 2s
