Video Editing Software: Choosing Your Learning Path

Compare the editing workflows taught at Quick Art: Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and EDIUS. Find a starting point for wedding video editing.

Anil Sharma
Founder & Lead Instructor
2026-04-30 7 min read

Start with the work you want to make

Choosing a tool is easier when you know your goal. A long wedding film, a short teaser and an album delivery each need a different workflow. Learn one editing environment carefully before trying to learn every application at once.

Premiere Pro: editing and storytelling

The academy’s curriculum uses Premiere Pro for project organisation, proxy workflows, timeline editing, transitions, pacing, motion graphics and the balance between dialogue and music.

DaVinci Resolve: colour and finishing

The Master Class covers primary colour controls, qualifiers, power windows and skin-tone evaluation, followed by an introduction to Fusion VFX and Fairlight audio work.

EDIUS: wedding editing workflows

The course introduces multi-track editing, multi-camera synchronisation, titles, music placement and exports for wedding delivery.

Make a small project, then review it

Practise with footage, finish an edit and review what you could improve. The 14-week Master Class connects these skills through a final wedding film project. Contact the academy to discuss the software and equipment required for your batch.
