DETAILED COURSE CURRICULUM

Your 4-week album design roadmap.

Photo editing, retouching, wedding layouts and print-ready delivery—organised into four weeks of practical learning.

WEEK 01Photo Editing & Album Foundations+

Prepare photographs for a coherent, polished album.

  • Photoshop, Lightroom and Camera Raw workflow; RAW processing, exposure and white balance.
  • Selections, masks, skin-tone correction and non-destructive editing.
WEEK 02Retouching & AI-Assisted Workflow+

Build consistency across the wedding photo collection.

  • Frequency separation, dodge and burn, texture preservation and creative backdrop work.
  • Batch processing, preset synchronisation and AI-assisted photo improvements.
WEEK 03Wedding Album Design & Storyboarding+

Create layouts that carry the wedding story.

  • Grids, margins, bleed, typography and template customisation.
  • Sequence Haldi, Mehndi, Varmala and Reception photographs into a connected album.
WEEK 04Album Finishing, Printing & Delivery+

Prepare final artwork and guide the client approval process.

  • Cover options, colour profiles and print preparation.
  • Proofing, digital approval, packaging and client presentation.
