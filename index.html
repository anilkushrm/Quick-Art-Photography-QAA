Is the training suitable for beginners?+

The flagship course starts with camera and editing fundamentals, then builds towards advanced workflows and a final wedding film. Tell us about your starting point so we can guide you.

Where does the Master Class take place?+

The 14-week Master Class is taught offline at Quick Art Photography Academy, Ayodhya Puri, near Lalit Bus Stand, Siwan, Bihar.

What is included in the 14-week programme?+

The programme includes 12 weeks of core technical training and 2 free weeks of business, marketing and AI growth. The supplied brochure includes accommodation for outstation students, practice resources and community mentorship.

Will I work on practical projects?+

Yes. The programme includes camera and lighting practice, wedding shoot simulations, photo and album assignments, video editing and a final wedding film with critique.

How do I confirm course fees and the next batch?+

Contact the academy on +91 9939800780 or submit a demo enquiry. The team can confirm current fees, payment options, the batch schedule and accommodation arrangements.

Does the course guarantee a job or income?+

No specific job or income is guaranteed. Training develops practical creative skills, with portfolio work, business learning and placement support described in the course brochure.
