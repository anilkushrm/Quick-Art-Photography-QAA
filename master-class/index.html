Course Structure

Our complete 14-Week offline Master Class curriculum in Siwan, Bihar. Master everything from camera optics and lighting to high-end retouching, EDIUS, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve color grading, and studio business automation.

14 Weeks Full Duration

PART 1 · PRODUCTION & CAMERA MASTERY
Week 01
Mirrorless Cameras, 3-Point Lighting & Gimbal Choreography
5 Sessions • Full Practical Workshop
[PRODUCTION] +

Hands-on offline training on Sony FX3, A7S III and Canon cinema mirrorless bodies. Master the exposure triangle, Log shooting, 3-point soft lighting, and butter-smooth DJI Ronin stabilization.

  • Camera Anatomy & Exposure Triangle Shutter Angle (180° Rule), Dual Native ISO optimization, and dynamic range preservation.
    Studio Gear
  • Sony S-Log3 & Canon C-Log3 Picture Profiles Exposing S-Log3 at +1.7 to +2.0 EV using false colors and custom display LUTs.
    Log Profiles
  • 3-Point Cinematic Lighting Setup Key light diffusers, rim/hair lights, background separation, and portable RGB tube choreography.
    Lighting Rig
  • DJI Ronin RS3/RS4 Balancing & Movement Choreography Eliminating vertical footstep bounce (Ninja walk), parallax reveals, 360 roll, and orbit shots.
    Gimbal Master
  • Couple Posing Psychology & Direction Directing non-model couples naturally for genuine laughter, intimate eye-lines, and elegant frames.
    Live Direction
PART 2 · HIGH-END PHOTO EDITING & ALBUM DESIGNING (WEEKS 02 - 06)
Week 02
Adobe Lightroom Classic & Batch RAW Development
4 Sessions•Fast Ingestion
[PHOTO EDITING] +

Culling 5,000+ RAW wedding photos in minutes, color calibration, smart collections, tone curve control, and creating custom studio preset packs.

Week 03
Photoshop High-End Retouching & Frequency Separation 2.0
4 Sessions•Pore-Preserved Skin
[HIGH-END RETOUCH] +

Frequency Separation 2.0 (16-bit math), Micro-Dodge & Burn, digital skin sculpting without blurring natural pores, and hair cleanup.

Week 04
AI Neural Filters, Generative Fill & Indian Bridal Grading
4 Sessions•Photoshop AI
[AI AUTOMATION] +

Adobe Firefly Generative Fill for extending mandap stages, removing crowd background distractors, AI neural facial harmonizing, and velvety bridal red dress grading.

Week 05
Modern Minimal Wedding Album Layout Design
4 Sessions•Canvera / Photokina Rules
[ALBUM LAYOUT] +

Grid math, white space rules, emotional ceremony flow (Haldi, Mehendi, Barat, Varmala, Phere, Vidai), and eliminating cluttered border templates.

Week 06
Album Finishing, CMYK Color Proofing & Luxury Cover Art
4 Sessions•Print Lab Ready
[FINISHING] +

Color space conversions (sRGB to Lab profile), spine width calculations, bleed margins, and designing premium acrylic glass & velvet leatherette box covers.

PART 3 · PROFESSIONAL VIDEO EDITING & COLOUR GRADING (WEEKS 07 - 12)
Week 07
EDIUS Pro 11 Fast Multicam Wedding Editing
4 Sessions•High Speed Delivery
[EDIUS PRO] +

Zero-transcoding timeline editing, real-time 4-camera live ritual switching, Quick Titler lower thirds, and rapid full-function same-day master rendering.

Week 08
Adobe Premiere Pro: Ingestion, Proxies & Multicam Waveforms
4 Sessions•Zero-Lag Ingestion
[PREMIERE PRO] +

ProRes proxy pipelines for smooth 4K 10-bit playback, automated multicam audio waveform sync, and master project bin management.

Week 09
Cinematic Storytelling Arc, Beat Sync & Emotional Pacing
4 Sessions•3-Act Narrative
[CINEMATIC] +

The 3-act emotional arc for wedding films. Speed ramping with optical flow, music stem dissection, and building grand bride entry teasers.

Week 10
Dialogue Cleaning, Foley Sound Design & Signature Wedding LUTs
4 Sessions•Audio & Lumetri
[SOUND & COLOR] +

Eliminating mic buzz and echo, layering whooshes, risers, and ambient nature textures. Primary Lumetri balancing and custom Indian wedding look LUTs.

Week 11
DaVinci Resolve 19 Studio: Node Architecture & Scopes
4 Sessions•Hollywood Grade
[COLOR GRADING] +

Serial, parallel, layer and keyer nodes. Waveform, vectorscope, and parade analysis. Multi-camera match between Sony, Canon, Panasonic, and drones.

Week 12
Kodak 2383 Film Emulation, HDR Wheels & Fairlight Delivery
4 Sessions•Film Emulation
[COLOR & FINISHING] +

CST color science workflow, halation, grain, Kodak 2383 print film look emulation, HDR wheels highlight compression, Fusion motion titles, and Fairlight audio mastering.

PART 4 · BUSINESS, MARKETING & STUDIO AUTOMATION (WEEKS 13 - 14)
Week 13
High-Ticket Studio Pricing, Client Contracts & Meta Ads
4 Sessions•Business Growth
[BUSINESS BLUEPRINT] +

How to charge ₹50,000 to ₹1,50,000+ per wedding shoot. Legal contracts, advance payment structures, and running targeted Meta (FB/Instagram) Ads for brides and families.

Week 14
Automated WhatsApp Inquiries, Lead Closing & Live Capstone
4 Sessions•Portfolio Defense
[CAPSTONE & AUTOMATION] +

Building automated 24/7 WhatsApp auto-responders with portfolio brochures, phone consultation objection handling scripts, and final capstone project presentation.
