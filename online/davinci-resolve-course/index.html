COURSE SYLLABUS

Step-by-Step Curriculum Roadmap

8 comprehensive modules designed to take you from beginner to professional mastery.

Module 01 DaVinci Interface, Color Management & ACES/YRGB Ingestion
+
Color page layout, scopes setup, project settings, ACES vs DaVinci YRGB Color Managed workflow, smooth playback setup.
Module 02 Primary Wheels, Log Wheels, Curves & Waveform Scopes
+
Master wheels, lift/gamma/gain, contrast, pivot, temperature, tint, and RGB parade / waveform monitoring.
Module 03 Node Tree Architecture & Workflow Organization
+
Serial, Parallel, Layer, Compound nodes, and clean order of operations for non-destructive grading without quality loss.
Module 04 Indian Wedding Skin Tone Matching on Vectorscope
+
Balancing yellow haldi and red stage lights, HSL qualifiers, skin tone indicator line calibration for natural Indian skin.
Module 05 Power Windows, 3D Tracking & Magic Mask
+
Subject isolation, face refinement, background desaturation, and dynamic tracking on moving couples during rituals.
Module 06 Multi-Camera Color Matching (Sony, Canon & Panasonic)
+
CST (Color Space Transform) matching, balancing footage shot on different cameras and varied lighting across the event.
Module 07 Film Emulation, Halation, Film Grain & Cinematic Wedding Looks
+
Vintage film look, Kodak 2383 emulation, custom highlight roll-off, glow, and rich cinematic mood styling.
Module 08 Creating, Exporting & Selling Custom 3D PowerGrades & LUTs
+
Generating .cube files, organizing gallery stills, exporting client deliverables in 4K DCI master format.
