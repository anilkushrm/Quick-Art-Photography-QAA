PROPOSED LEARNING OUTLINE

Digital Marketing Course: from foundations to a finished project.

MODULE 01Define the offer+

Describe the customer problem, service boundaries and a clear reason to enquire.

MODULE 02Understand the audience+

Use relevant needs and context to guide messaging without assuming every viewer is a buyer.

MODULE 03Plan content and creative+

Build a small set of messages and visuals to test rather than one unsupported promise.

MODULE 04Connect campaigns to enquiries+

Align ads, landing pages and contact steps; understand budget and account prerequisites.

MODULE 05Read campaign signals+

Distinguish reach, clicks and enquiries and recognise the limits of attribution.

MODULE 06Review and iterate+

Keep a test log, compare meaningful outcomes and decide what to improve next.

PRACTICAL PROJECT

Make the learning visible.

Prepare a campaign brief for a studio service, with an audience hypothesis, creative concepts, landing-page outline and measurement plan.

LEARNING OUTCOME

Explain your choices.

Use the outline to assess your ability to define the offer, understand the audience, plan content and creative. Keep a record of revisions and the reasons behind them.
