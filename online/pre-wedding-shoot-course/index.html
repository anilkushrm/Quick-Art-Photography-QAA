ONLINE PROGRAM · Pre-Wedding Shoot Course

Plan the story before the first frame.

Explore preparation, location planning, available light and natural direction for a pre-wedding shoot with a clear visual story.

  • Plan locations and timing
  • Work with natural light

WHO IT IS FOR

A useful next step for your workflow.

Wedding photographers, camera operators and filmmakers planning couple shoots.

TOOLS & PREPARATION

Start with the right setup.

Camera, suitable lenses and available-light or lighting equipment; confirm equipment requirements before enrolling.

Bring a clear learning goal. Ask about hardware, software licences and required prior knowledge before enrolling.

PROPOSED LEARNING OUTLINE

Pre-Wedding Shoot Course: from foundations to a finished project.

MODULE 01Develop the brief+

Discuss the couple’s preferences, the intended film and a realistic scope for the shoot.

MODULE 02Plan locations and timing+

Consider access, permissions, light, travel and a weather backup.

MODULE 03Work with natural light+

Evaluate direction and contrast and choose camera positions that suit the scene.

MODULE 04Direct people comfortably+

Use clear prompts and movement rather than relying on rigid poses.

MODULE 05Capture editorial coverage+

Plan wide, medium and detail shots and maintain useful screen direction.

MODULE 06Review and hand over+

Back up footage, annotate useful takes and check coverage against the intended story.

PRACTICAL PROJECT

Make the learning visible.

Prepare a location brief, mood board and shot list for a short couple film, then review how the planned shots connect in an edit.

LEARNING OUTCOME

Explain your choices.

Use the outline to assess your ability to develop the brief, plan locations and timing, work with natural light. Keep a record of revisions and the reasons behind them.
