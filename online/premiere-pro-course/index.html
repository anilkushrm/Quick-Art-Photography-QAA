COURSE SYLLABUS

Step-by-Step Curriculum Roadmap

9 comprehensive modules designed to take you from complete beginner to master wedding video editor.

Module 01 Interface, Project Setup & Proxy Workflow
+
Premiere Pro interface tour, project panel, source monitor, timeline settings, 4K 10-bit ingestion, smooth ProRes proxy creation without computer lag, and media organization.
Module 02 Multi-Camera Setup & Multi-Audio Waveform Sync
+
Multi-cam sequence creation, angle switching in real time, synchronizing lapel mics with camera sound, and managing heavy multicam wedding rituals.
Module 03 Cinematic Cutting, Story Pacing & Invisible Transitions
+
J-cuts, L-cuts, speed ramping, whip pans, match cuts, emotion-driven pacing, and building a cohesive narrative for bride & groom entries.
Module 04 Dialogue Audio Cleaning & Dynamic Sound Design
+
Background noise removal, audio ducking under speech, EQ & compression for crystal clear vows, sound effects layering (whooshes, risers, ambient texture).
Module 05 Lumetri Color Grading & Natural Indian Skin Tones
+
Primary correction, white balance matching between Sony, Canon & Panasonic cameras, HSL secondary qualifiers, LUT workflow, and filmic warm tones.
Module 06 AI Editing Workflow in Adobe Premiere Pro
+
Text-based transcript editing, auto subtitle generation with Hindi font formatting, AI voice clarity, auto reframe for multi-platform delivery.
Module 07 Complete 1-Minute Wedding Teaser Project
+
Step-by-step editing of an authentic high-budget wedding teaser from scratch using raw footage, music selection, and color grading.
Module 08 Instagram Reels & YouTube 4K Retention Secrets
+
Vertical editing workflow, retention hooks, motion typography, trending audio beats, and quick export presets.
Module 09 Professional Export, Archiving & Client Delivery
+
Bitrate calculations, ProRes & H.264/H.265 master exports, YouTube optimal settings, client pendrive structuring, and project backup strategies.
