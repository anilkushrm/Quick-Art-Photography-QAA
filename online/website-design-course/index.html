ONLINE PROGRAM · Website Design Course

Build a clear home for your creative work.

Explore how structure, readable design and purposeful enquiry journeys turn a portfolio into a useful website for a photographer or studio.

  • Organise pages and content
  • Create a visual system

PROPOSED LEARNING OUTLINE

Website Design Course: from foundations to a finished project.

MODULE 01Map the visitor journey+

Define who the website serves and the information needed before an enquiry.

MODULE 02Organise pages and content+

Plan services, portfolio, about and contact content without repetitive copy.

MODULE 03Create a visual system+

Choose readable type, a restrained palette and reusable buttons and cards.

MODULE 04Design for small screens+

Prioritise content, touch targets and images across common device widths.

MODULE 05Connect enquiry and search+

Understand form validation, descriptive metadata and useful internal links.

MODULE 06Prepare for launch+

Check links, image weight, accessibility, backups and hosting responsibilities.

PRACTICAL PROJECT

Make the learning visible.

Plan a studio website with a focused homepage, project gallery and accessible enquiry form, including mobile layouts and a launch checklist.

LEARNING OUTCOME

Explain your choices.

Use the outline to assess your ability to map the visitor journey, organise pages and content, create a visual system. Keep a record of revisions and the reasons behind them.
