Shipping & Delivery Policy

How we deliver our digital courses and physical materials

📅 Last Updated: September 17, 2026
⚡ Instant Digital Delivery: All online courses are delivered digitally and instantly upon successful payment. There is no physical shipping involved for digital products. Access is available 24/7 from any device.

Quick Art Photography Academy offers primarily digital educational products (online courses) and offline campus training. The delivery methods differ for each:

💻

Online Digital Courses (LMS)

Video lessons, notes, resources, and quizzes delivered via our secure Student Portal. Accessible from any device — phone, tablet, or computer.

🗓️ On Day 1

2. Digital Course Delivery Details

2.1 Access Timeline

  • Immediate: Course access is granted within seconds of successful payment confirmation from Razorpay
  • Portal Login: Access your courses at quickartphotography.in/portal/
  • Login Method: Enter your registered mobile number → receive OTP → access dashboard

2.2 System Requirements

  • Internet connection (minimum 1 Mbps for smooth video playback, 5+ Mbps recommended)
  • Modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge — latest 2 versions)
  • Mobile (Android 8+, iOS 13+) or Desktop/Laptop
  • Cookies and JavaScript must be enabled

2.3 Content Availability

  • Course videos are streamed securely via Bunny.net CDN — no downloads allowed (anti-piracy)
  • Course materials (notes, PDFs, resources) may be downloadable where provided
  • Content is available 24/7 with occasional maintenance windows (communicated in advance)
  • Lifetime access to purchased course content

3. Delivery Issues / Access Problems

If you experience any issues accessing your course after payment:

  1. Check your internet connection
  2. Try logging out and back in at the Student Portal
  3. Clear browser cache and cookies
  4. If the issue persists, contact us with your Razorpay Payment ID and registered mobile number

We resolve all access issues within 24 hours of receiving your report.

4. No Physical Shipping Policy

Quick Art Photography Academy does not ship any physical goods for online course purchases. There are no shipping charges, no tracking numbers, and no physical delivery for digital courses. The "shipping" in payment gateway terminology refers to our digital delivery policy.

5. Geographic Availability

  • Online Courses: Available to students across India and internationally. Payments accepted in Indian Rupees (INR) via Razorpay.
  • Campus Courses: Physical attendance required at Siwan, Bihar, India. Students from outside Siwan are responsible for their own travel and accommodation arrangements.
